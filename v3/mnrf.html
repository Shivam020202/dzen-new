CLINICAL OVERVIEW

MNRF Treatment

MNRF (Microneedling Radiofrequency) combines microneedling with radiofrequency energy. Fine needles deliver controlled heat into the deeper layers of the skin, helping stimulate collagen and improve the appearance of acne scars, enlarged pores, uneven texture and early laxity.

Settings such as needle depth and energy are adjusted for each area and skin type. A doctor's assessment helps determine whether MNRF is suitable for your concerns.

THE TREATMENT

Understanding MNRF

Standard microneedling creates controlled micro-injuries to prompt skin repair. MNRF adds radiofrequency heat at the needle tips, which can strengthen the collagen response in the dermis, where scars and laxity originate.

Because insulated needles deliver most of the heat below the surface, MNRF can be a suitable option for many Indian skin types. Pigmentation risk is still assessed individually, and aftercare — especially sun protection — remains important.

CLINICAL PROCESS

Process of MNRF Treatment

Assessment & Numbing

Your skin and scar type are assessed, then a numbing cream is applied to keep the treatment comfortable.

Sterile Preparation

The skin is cleansed with antiseptic, and a sterile, single-use needle cartridge is used for every session.

MNRF Passes

The handpiece is applied across the skin, with depth and energy adjusted for each area, such as deeper settings for cheek scars.

Soothing & Aftercare

Calming products are applied. Redness typically settles within 24–48 hours, and clear aftercare guidance is provided.

REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS

What Results Can You Expect?

Changes are gradual. Texture and pores may begin to look more refined after the first few sessions, while scar improvement builds as new collagen forms over the following months.

A course of 3–6 sessions, about four to six weeks apart, is commonly recommended. Results vary with scar type, skin condition and individual healing.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How is MNRF different from microneedling?
+
Standard microneedling relies on controlled micro-injury alone. MNRF adds radiofrequency heat at the needle tips, which can strengthen the collagen response, making it useful for deeper scars and skin tightening.
Is MNRF suitable for Indian skin?
+
Insulated needles deliver most of the heat beneath the skin surface, so MNRF can be suitable for many Indian skin types. Your skin is still assessed individually, and sun protection is important afterwards.
Does MNRF hurt?
+
With numbing cream it is usually well tolerated. You may feel pressure and a brief, warm prickling sensation as the handpiece is applied.
How many sessions are needed for acne scars?
+
For moderate to severe acne scars, a course of three to six sessions, spaced four to six weeks apart, is commonly recommended. Your plan is set after assessment.
What is the downtime?
+
The skin usually looks red, similar to mild sunburn, for 24–48 hours. Tiny grid marks may be visible for a few days. Aftercare advice, including when to resume makeup, is provided.
Who should avoid MNRF?
+
MNRF is generally avoided with active inflammatory acne, a tendency to keloid scarring, active skin infection, during pregnancy, or with a pacemaker or implanted electronic device.
