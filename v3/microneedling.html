CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Microneedling Treatment

Microneedling is a minimally invasive skin treatment that uses fine needles to create controlled micro-injuries in the skin. This process stimulates the skin’s natural repair response and supports collagen production, helping improve the appearance of uneven texture and selected skin concerns.

The treatment can be customised according to your skin condition, concerns and desired results. A professional assessment helps determine whether microneedling is appropriate for your skin.

THE TREATMENT

Understanding Microneedling

Microneedling works by creating tiny, controlled punctures in the skin using a specialised device. These micro-injuries encourage the body’s natural healing process and can stimulate collagen and elastin production.

As the skin repairs itself, it may gradually become smoother and more even-looking. The treatment can be adjusted according to the area being treated and the condition of the skin.

CLINICAL PROCESS

Process of Microneedling Treatment

Skin Assessment & Planning

Your skin’s condition, texture and concerns are examined, and the treatment depth is chosen for your skin and desired outcome.

Skin Preparation

The skin is cleansed and prepared before treatment, and a topical numbing cream may be used when appropriate to keep you comfortable.

Controlled Microneedling

A specialised device is moved across the treatment area to create controlled micro-channels that stimulate the skin’s natural repair.

Recovery & Aftercare

Temporary redness may occur as the skin repairs. Skincare and sun protection advice is given, and follow-up guides further sessions.

OUTCOMES & BENEFITS

Benefits of Microneedling Treatment

Microneedling may help improve the overall appearance and texture of the skin. Depending on the individual’s condition, potential benefits include:

Improved skin texture
Smoother-looking skin
Reduced appearance of selected acne scars
Improvement in uneven-looking skin
Support for collagen production
Reduced appearance of fine lines
More refined-looking pores
Improved overall skin quality

The extent of improvement varies depending on the skin condition, treatment depth and individual healing response.

REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS

What Results Can You Expect?

Microneedling can gradually improve the appearance and texture of the skin as collagen remodeling takes place. You may notice smoother-looking skin, improved texture and a more even appearance.

Results develop progressively and may become more noticeable after multiple sessions. The number of treatments required depends on the concern being addressed and the individual’s response.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is microneedling?
+
Microneedling is a minimally invasive treatment that uses fine needles to create controlled micro-injuries in the skin and stimulate its natural repair response.
Is microneedling painful?
+
A topical numbing cream may be used before treatment to improve comfort. Some people may experience mild sensations during the procedure.
How many microneedling sessions are needed?
+
The number of sessions depends on the skin concern, its severity and the desired outcome. A personalised treatment schedule can be recommended after assessment.
Is there downtime after microneedling?
+
Some redness, sensitivity or mild swelling may occur after treatment. Recovery varies depending on the treatment depth and individual skin response.
