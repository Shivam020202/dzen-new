PRE-BRIDAL PROGRAMME

Looking Like Yourself, in Balance

THE JOURNEY

The D'Zen Bridal Journey

A wedding is the one day you will be looked at most closely — and the worst possible time to try something new. Our programme starts early and moves slowly, so that what arrives on the day is recognisably you.

Nothing here is a package bought off a menu. We read your skin, your hair and your wellbeing as one picture, then build a plan that restores balance rather than chasing a look. Everything is timed backwards from your date, with a settling window built in before every event.

01 Consultation & Diagnosis A full assessment of skin, hair and lifestyle, plus imaging so progress is measured rather than guessed.
02 A Plan Timed to Your Date Sequenced backwards from the wedding, with recovery and settling windows placed before every function.
03 Review, Adjust, Hold Regular reviews to adjust what is working, and a maintenance plan so the result holds through the season.
CHOOSE YOUR RUNWAY

Three, Six or Nine Months

Skin works on its own schedule. The more runway you give it, the less has to be done to it — which is why the longer plans are also the gentler ones.

3 Months · The Polish

For brides already in good condition who want clarity, glow and a calm finish. Focused on surface and shine, with nothing that needs long recovery.

  • Month 3 Diagnosis, barrier repair and a corrected home routine.
  • Month 2 Medifacials and gentle resurfacing, spaced three weeks apart.
  • Month 1 Hydration and glow protocols; hair and scalp conditioning.
  • Final 2 Weeks Settling only — no new actives, no new treatments.
Most Chosen 6 Months · The Programme

Enough runway to correct as well as polish. Pigmentation, texture and hair fall can all be addressed properly, with room to adjust as results come through.

  • Month 6 Full diagnosis and imaging; barrier and routine reset.
  • Months 5 – 4 Active correction — pigmentation, texture, acne or scarring.
  • Month 3 Collagen stimulation and hair protocols begin; mid-point review.
  • Month 2 Refinement and body work; any injectables placed here at the latest.
  • Month 1 Glow, hydration and rehearsal of the final-week routine.
  • Final 2 Weeks Settling only — nothing new introduced.
9 Months · The Full Arc

The most conservative route, and the one we recommend where concerns are long-standing. Slower protocols, longer gaps, and time to change course without pressure.

  • Months 9 – 8 Diagnosis, bloodwork where indicated, and internal groundwork.
  • Months 7 – 5 Deep correction at an unhurried pace; hair restoration begins.
  • Month 4 Review against baseline imaging; plan adjusted on evidence.
  • Months 3 – 2 Collagen work, body protocols and refinement.
  • Month 1 Glow, calm and the final-week routine rehearsed.
  • Final 2 Weeks Settling only — nothing new introduced.
WHAT WE WORK ON

Skin, Hair, Body, Wellness

Four strands of the same plan. Treating any one of them in isolation is what makes bridal results look applied rather than earned.

Skin

Clarity and even tone first; glow is what happens once the barrier is working properly.

  • Pigmentation and melasma
  • Texture, pores and scarring
  • Active acne management
  • Collagen stimulation

Hair

Density and scalp health take months to shift, so hair work starts on day one.

  • Hair fall diagnosis
  • PRP and GFC protocols
  • Scalp barrier and dandruff
  • Pre-event conditioning

Body

The areas a lehenga or gown actually shows — back, arms, neck and décolletage.

  • Back and body clarity
  • Laser hair reduction
  • Neck and décolletage care
  • Contouring where indicated

Wellness

Sleep, stress and nutrition decide how much of the clinic work actually holds.

  • Nutrition and gut support
  • Sleep and cortisol
  • Bloodwork where indicated
  • Calm protocols before events
