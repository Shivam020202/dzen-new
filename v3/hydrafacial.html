hydrafacial in Delhi & Gurgaon

THE TREATMENT

What a Hydrafacial Actually Is

A Hydrafacial is a patented, multi-step facial treatment that uses a unique vortex-fusion delivery system. It cleanses, exfoliates, extracts impurities, and infuses the skin with intensive serums.

Unlike traditional facials that rely on manual extraction, Hydrafacial uses gentle vacuum technology to clear pores while simultaneously pushing hydrating and brightening active ingredients deep into the skin.

Medical-grade, not a salon peel

The difference is concentration, pH and judgement. A clinical peel runs at strengths that can genuinely shift pigmentation, which also means the agent has to be matched to your Fitzpatrick type and the endpoint watched rather than timed by a clock. That is the whole reason it is done by a dermatologist.

Chosen for Indian skin specifically

Deeper skin tones respond well to peels and carry a higher risk of post-inflammatory pigmentation if pushed too hard. We work with that, not against it: gentler agents, priming beforehand, and depth built across a course. Restraint here is not caution — it is what makes the result hold.

HOW IT WORKS

One Action, Four Effects

A peel is a single, controlled event, but it sets four separate things in motion — each landing at a different point and reaching a different depth. Knowing which arrives when is what makes the waiting bearable.

Step 1

Cleanse & Peel

Gentle exfoliation and relaxing resurfacing to uncover a new layer of skin.

Step 2

Extract & Hydrate

Remove debris from pores with painless suction. Nourish with intense moisturizers.

Step 3

Fuse & Protect

Saturate the skin’s surface with antioxidants and peptides to maximize your glow.

Ongoing

Maintained Clarity

With monthly sessions, pores remain clear and the skin barrier stays deeply hydrated.

SUITABILITY

Who It Is For

The honest version. If a peel is not the right answer for what you are seeing, we would rather establish that in the consultation than three sessions in.

A Good Fit If

  • You want an immediate glow for an event
  • You struggle with blackheads and congested pores
  • Your skin feels dehydrated and looks dull
  • You want a zero-downtime lunchtime procedure

We Would Wait If

  • You have active, inflammatory cystic acne
  • You are looking for deep scar revision
  • You have a fresh sunburn or rash

EXPECTED RESULTS

What Happens, and When

The first fortnight of a peel course rarely looks like progress. This is the sequence, so that nothing in it comes as a surprise.

Typical course of a superficial to medium peel. Individual response varies with skin type, the agent used and how consistently sun protection is worn.
Stage What Shows What It Means
Days 1–2 Tightness and a faint bronzing The skin feels tight and may look slightly darker before it looks better. This is expected and is not the result.
Days 3–5 Flaking, if it comes at all Superficial peels often produce no visible shedding whatsoever. Where it does happen it is fine and powdery rather than sheet-like.
Week 2 First clear read Tone looks more even and the skin handles light better. This is the point at which most people notice, rather than immediately after the session.
Sessions 3–4 Marks measurably lighter Post-acne pigmentation lifts cumulatively. This is where the course earns its place, and why a single peel is rarely worth booking.
Month 3 onward Texture and firmness The collagen response arrives last. It is the least dramatic change and the one that holds longest with maintenance.
BEFORE YOU BOOK

hydrafacial FAQs

Is Hydrafacial good for acne?

It is excellent for mild acne, blackheads, and congestion. The salicylic acid blend and vacuum extraction clear pores effectively. However, we avoid passing over active, inflamed cysts.

Does it hurt?

Not at all. Most patients describe it as a cool, refreshing sensation, like a wet paintbrush moving across the face. There is no painful manual squeezing.

Can I get it before a big event?

Yes. Hydrafacial is the ultimate "red carpet" treatment. It leaves your skin glowing and plump immediately, making it perfect 1-2 days before an event.

How often should I get one?

For optimal skin health, we recommend one session every 4 weeks. It acts as a deep clean to reset your skin for the month ahead.

What is the downtime?

There is absolutely no downtime. You can apply makeup and return to normal activities immediately, though we recommend letting the serums soak in for the rest of the day.
