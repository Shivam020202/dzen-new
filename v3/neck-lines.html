Neck Lines (Tech Neck)

CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Neck Lines

Neck lines are horizontal creases or vertical bands that appear on the neck. Horizontal lines are often linked to repeatedly looking down at screens, sometimes called tech neck, while vertical bands and loose skin tend to develop with age.

The skin on the neck is thinner than on the face and is often left out of daily skincare and sun protection. A personalised assessment helps determine which approach suits your neck.

WHY IT HAPPENS

What Causes Neck Lines?

Neck lines usually develop from a mix of posture, skin ageing and sun exposure. These are the most common factors.

Looking Down at Screens

Bending the neck to look at phones and laptops creases the skin in the same place for hours every day.

Thin Skin & Collagen Loss

Neck skin is thinner than facial skin and loses collagen over time, so lines form and deepen more easily.

Sun Damage

The neck is often missed with sunscreen, and sun exposure speeds up the breakdown of collagen and elastin.

Muscle Bands

With age, the platysma muscle can form visible vertical bands that pull the neck and jawline downward.

CARING FOR YOUR SKIN

Skin Care Do’s & Don’ts

Daily habits can help slow new neck lines from forming and protect your treatment results.

Do

  • Hold your phone at eye level
  • Apply sunscreen to your neck every day
  • Extend skincare and moisturiser to the neck
  • Take regular posture breaks from screens

Avoid

  • Looking down at screens for long periods
  • Sleeping on very high pillows
  • Leaving the neck out of sun protection
  • Harsh scrubs on thin neck skin

When to See a Doctor

If neck lines or loose skin are affecting your confidence, or neck bands are becoming more prominent, a dermatologist can assess the cause and recommend suitable non-surgical options.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about neck lines and tech neck.

What causes neck lines?
+
Neck lines are caused by a combination of repeated neck bending, thinner skin, collagen loss with age, sun damage and, in some people, prominent neck muscle bands.
Can neck lines be treated without surgery?
+
Yes. Mild to moderate neck lines and laxity can often be improved with non- surgical treatments such as HIFU, MNRF, mesotherapy or anti-wrinkle injections.
Does Botox help neck lines?
+
Botox can soften vertical neck bands by relaxing the platysma muscle. Horizontal lines usually respond better to skin-quality and tightening treatments.
How many sessions are needed?
+
This depends on the treatment and how deep the lines are. Most plans involve a course of sessions, which your doctor will outline at assessment.
Is there downtime?
+
Most neck treatments have little downtime. Mild redness, swelling or small bumps can occur and usually settle within a day or two.
Can neck lines be prevented?
+
Good posture, holding screens at eye level, daily sunscreen and moisturising the neck can all help slow new lines from forming.
