CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Mesolipolysis Treatment

Mesolipolysis is a minimally invasive aesthetic treatment used to target localised areas of unwanted fat. It involves the use of carefully selected injectable substances into specific areas to support the breakdown and reduction of fat cells.

The treatment is designed for selected areas where stubborn fat may remain despite healthy lifestyle habits. A professional assessment is important to determine suitability and create an appropriate treatment plan.

THE TREATMENT

Understanding Mesolipolysis

Mesolipolysis focuses on small, localised areas of fat rather than overall weight reduction. The treatment works by delivering selected substances into targeted areas, where they can affect fat cells and support their gradual reduction.

It may be considered for areas where diet and exercise have not produced the desired contouring effect. The appropriate treatment approach depends on the area being treated, the amount of localised fat and individual goals.

CLINICAL PROCESS

Process of Mesolipolysis Treatment

Body Assessment & Planning

The area is examined for localised fat and skin condition, and the number of sessions and approach are planned around your needs.

Treatment Preparation

The selected area is carefully cleaned and prepared before the procedure, so the formulation can be administered safely and precisely.

Targeted Injections

The selected formulation is carefully administered into the targeted area using precise injection techniques suited to your body.

Aftercare & Follow-Up

The area is monitored and aftercare advice is given, with follow-up visits to assess your response and plan any further sessions.

OUTCOMES & BENEFITS

Benefits of Mesolipolysis Treatment

When appropriately selected, mesolipolysis may help improve the appearance of localised fat deposits and body contours. Potential benefits include:

Targeted treatment of localised fat
Improved appearance of selected body areas
Non-surgical approach to body contouring
Treatment tailored to individual areas
Gradual improvement in body contours
No major surgical incision

The extent of improvement varies between individuals and depends on factors such as the treatment area, amount of fat and individual response.

REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS

What Results Can You Expect?

The treatment aims to gradually improve the appearance of selected areas with localised fat. Changes may become more noticeable over time as the body processes the affected fat cells.

Results vary depending on the treatment area, amount of localised fat, number of sessions and individual response. Mesolipolysis is not intended to replace weight-loss treatment or a healthy lifestyle.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is mesolipolysis?
+
Mesolipolysis is an injectable aesthetic treatment designed to target selected areas of localised fat and support body contouring.
Is mesolipolysis a weight-loss treatment?
+
No. It is intended for targeted areas of localised fat and is not a substitute for weight-loss management, exercise or a balanced diet.
Which areas can be treated?
+
Potential treatment areas depend on the specific formulation and clinical approach used. Suitability should be assessed individually.
Is mesolipolysis painful?
+
Some discomfort, stinging or tenderness may occur during or after the injections. The experience varies between individuals and treatment areas.
Is there any downtime?
+
Temporary swelling, redness, tenderness or bruising may occur following treatment. Recovery varies according to the individual and treated area.
How many sessions are required?
+
The number of sessions depends on the treatment area, amount of localised fat and individual response. A personalised plan can be discussed during consultation.
When will I see results?
+
Results are generally gradual rather than immediate. The body needs time to respond to the treatment and process the affected fat cells.
Is mesolipolysis suitable for everyone?
+
No. Suitability depends on factors such as the treatment area, overall health, skin condition and individual expectations. A professional consultation is recommended before treatment.
