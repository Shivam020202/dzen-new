HOW WE TEACH

Training Philosophy

Technique is the easy half. What takes longer to teach is the judgement that keeps a result in balance — and that is what these sessions are built around.

01

Diagnose Before You Treat

Every session starts with assessment, not injection. If you cannot explain why a face reads the way it does, no technique will fix it.

02

Restraint Is a Skill

Knowing when to stop is harder than knowing how to proceed, and it is the single clearest difference between a natural result and an obvious one.

03

Safety Is Non-Negotiable

Anatomy, danger zones and complication management are covered before any hands-on work — every time, regardless of the group's experience.

04

Small Groups, Real Cases

Numbers are capped so every participant works on live cases under direct supervision, rather than watching from the back of a room.
