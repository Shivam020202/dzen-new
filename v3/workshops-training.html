HOW WE TEACH

Training Philosophy

Technique is the easy half. What takes longer to teach is the judgement that keeps a result in balance — and that is what every session is built around.

Diagnose Before You Treat

Every session starts with assessment, not injection. If you cannot explain why a face reads the way it does, no technique will fix it.

Restraint Is a Skill

Knowing when to stop is harder than knowing how to proceed — and it is the clearest difference between a natural result and an obvious one.

Safety Is Non-Negotiable

Anatomy, danger zones and complication management are covered before any hands-on work, every time, regardless of the group's experience.

Small Groups, Real Cases

Numbers are capped so every participant works on live cases under direct supervision, rather than watching from the back of a room.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What to know before you register for a workshop.

Who can attend the workshops?
+
The workshops are open to registered medical practitioners — dermatologists, plastic surgeons, aesthetic physicians and dentists practising aesthetics. Each listing states its level, and foundation sessions assume no prior injectable experience.
Is the training hands-on?
+
Yes. Every workshop includes supervised work on live cases. Group sizes are capped so that each participant treats rather than observes.
Do participants receive a certificate?
+
Yes. Participants who complete the full programme receive a certificate of attendance from D'Zen Derma.
How is my place confirmed?
+
Submit the registration form and our team will contact you to confirm availability, share the fee and joining details, and complete the booking.
Can you run a workshop for our clinic or institution?
+
Dr. Aarushi accepts a limited number of invitations for in-house training, conference sessions and fellowship modules. Use the registration form and choose the private or institutional training option.
