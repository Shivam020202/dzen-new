CLINICAL OVERVIEW

miraDry Sweat Reduction

miraDry is a non-surgical treatment for excessive underarm sweating. It uses controlled microwave energy to target and reduce sweat and odour glands in the underarm, helping provide a long-term reduction in sweating rather than temporary relief.

Because treated glands do not usually regenerate, results are designed to be long- lasting. A doctor's assessment confirms whether miraDry is suitable for your concern.

THE TREATMENT

Understanding miraDry

Excessive underarm sweating, known as axillary hyperhidrosis, can affect comfort, clothing choices and confidence. Antiperspirants work on the skin surface and need daily use, while miraDry works on the sweat glands themselves.

The body has millions of sweat glands elsewhere, so treating the underarms does not affect your ability to cool down. If sweating is sudden, new or affects the whole body, an underlying cause is checked first.

CLINICAL PROCESS

Process of miraDry Treatment

Assessment

Your sweating pattern and medical history are assessed to confirm miraDry is the right option for you.

Mapping & Numbing

The underarm area is marked with a treatment template, and local anaesthetic is given to keep you comfortable.

miraDry Treatment

The handpiece delivers controlled energy to the sweat-gland layer while cooling protects the skin surface.

Aftercare

Swelling, soreness or tenderness are common for a few days. Cold packs and simple aftercare advice help recovery.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to reduce sweating in the underarms?
+
Yes. The underarms contain only a small proportion of the body's sweat glands, so the body continues to regulate its temperature normally.
Does miraDry hurt?
+
Local anaesthetic is given before treatment, so most people feel little discomfort during the procedure. Some soreness and swelling afterwards is common.
How many sessions will I need?
+
Many people see a meaningful result after one session. A second session may be recommended for further reduction.
What is the downtime?
+
Most people return to daily activities within a day or two. Swelling, tenderness or numbness in the underarm can last from a few days to a few weeks.
Will it reduce underarm odour?
+
miraDry also targets odour glands in the underarm, so many people notice reduced odour along with reduced sweating.
Who is miraDry not suitable for?
+
It is not recommended during pregnancy, for people with a pacemaker or implanted electronic device, or where sweating is caused by an underlying medical condition that needs treatment first.
