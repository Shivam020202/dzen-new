Droopy Eyelids

CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Droopy Eyelids

Droopy or hooded eyelids happen when the skin of the upper eyelid becomes looser or the brows sit lower. This can make the eyes look tired, heavy or smaller, and in some cases can affect vision.

Mild heaviness can often be improved without surgery, while more significant drooping may need a surgical opinion. A personalised assessment helps decide which approach is right for you.

WHY IT HAPPENS

What Causes Droopy Eyelids?

Eyelid heaviness usually develops gradually. These are the most common factors.

Skin Laxity

With age, eyelid skin loses collagen and elasticity, so it stretches and can fold over the lid.

Lowering Brows

As the forehead and brows descend over time, they push extra skin down onto the upper eyelid.

Genetics

Some people naturally have hooded eyelids or are prone to heaviness at a younger age.

Muscle Weakness

In some cases, weakness of the muscle that lifts the lid causes drooping that needs medical review.

CARING FOR YOUR SKIN

Skin Care Do’s & Don’ts

Gentle care for the eye area helps protect your skin and support your results.

Do

  • Use sunscreen and sunglasses outdoors
  • Remove eye makeup gently every night
  • Get regular, good-quality sleep
  • Mention any changes in your vision

Avoid

  • Rubbing or pulling the eyelids
  • Sleeping in eye makeup
  • Smoking, which speeds up skin ageing
  • Ignoring sudden drooping of one eyelid

When to See a Doctor

If one eyelid droops suddenly, drooping affects your vision, or it comes with other symptoms such as double vision or weakness, see a doctor promptly, as this needs medical assessment.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about droopy eyelids.

What causes droopy eyelids?
+
Droopy eyelids are commonly caused by skin laxity with age, lowering brows and genetics. Less commonly, muscle or nerve problems are involved.
Can droopy eyelids be treated without surgery?
+
Mild heaviness can often be improved with a Botox brow lift, HIFU or MNRF. Significant excess skin usually needs a surgical opinion.
What is a Botox brow lift?
+
It uses small, precise injections to relax muscles that pull the brows down, allowing them to lift slightly and open up the eyes.
How long do results last?
+
A Botox brow lift usually lasts three to four months. Skin-tightening results from HIFU or MNRF build over months and can last longer.
Is there downtime?
+
Downtime is usually minimal, with mild redness or swelling that settles quickly.
When should I see a surgeon?
+
If excess skin is heavy, affects your vision, or non-surgical options are unlikely to help, your doctor will recommend a surgical opinion.
