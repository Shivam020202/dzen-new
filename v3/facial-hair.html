CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Unwanted Facial Hair

Unwanted facial hair is common and can appear on the upper lip, chin, jawline, cheeks or sides of the face. For some people it is fine and light, while for others it is darker, coarser or grows back quickly after shaving or threading.

Sometimes facial hair is linked to hormones, so the cause matters as much as the treatment. A personalised assessment helps decide whether further checks are needed and which option suits your skin and hair type.

WHY IT HAPPENS

What Causes Unwanted Facial Hair?

Facial hair growth is influenced by genetics and hormones. These are the most common factors.

Genetics

The amount, colour and thickness of facial hair are partly inherited and vary between families and ethnic backgrounds.

Hormonal Changes

Higher androgen levels can increase facial hair, especially on the chin and jawline.

PCOS

Polycystic ovary syndrome is a common cause of new or increasing coarse facial hair in women.

Medications & Life Stages

Some medicines, pregnancy and menopause can change hair growth patterns on the face.

CARING FOR YOUR SKIN

Skin Care Do’s & Don’ts

The right habits between sessions protect your skin and support your results.

Do

  • Shave the area before laser sessions if advised
  • Use sunscreen every day
  • Keep to your planned session schedule
  • Mention any new or sudden hair growth

Avoid

  • Waxing, threading or plucking during laser treatment
  • Sun exposure or tanning before sessions
  • Bleaching creams on irritated skin
  • Ignoring sudden increases in hair growth

When to See a Doctor

If facial hair appears suddenly, increases quickly, or comes with irregular periods, acne or weight changes, see a doctor, as these can be signs of a hormonal condition such as PCOS.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about unwanted facial hair.

What causes unwanted facial hair in women?
+
Common causes include genetics, hormonal changes and conditions such as PCOS. Some medicines and life stages like menopause can also play a part.
Is laser hair reduction permanent?
+
Laser provides long-term hair reduction rather than permanent removal. Most people see significantly less, finer hair, with occasional maintenance sessions.
How many laser sessions will I need?
+
Facial hair usually needs a course of six to eight sessions or more, spaced a few weeks apart. Hormonal hair may need ongoing maintenance.
Is laser safe for Indian skin?
+
Modern diode lasers can be used safely on many Indian skin types when settings are chosen carefully by a trained professional.
Can I shave between sessions?
+
Yes. Shaving is usually fine and is often advised before sessions. Waxing, threading and plucking should be avoided during a laser course.
Should I see a doctor about facial hair?
+
Yes, if growth is sudden or comes with other symptoms such as irregular periods or acne, as it may indicate a hormonal condition.
