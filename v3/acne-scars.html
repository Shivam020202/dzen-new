CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Acne Scars

Acne scars are lasting changes that can develop after acne-related inflammation affects the deeper layers of the skin. They may appear as depressed areas, uneven texture or changes in skin colour, and can affect the overall appearance of the skin even after breakouts have healed.

Not every mark left after acne is a true scar. Some are temporary pigmentation, while others involve changes in skin structure. A personalised assessment identifies your scar type and the most appropriate way to improve it.

WHY IT HAPPENS

What Causes Acne Scars?

Scarring depends on how deep the inflammation reached and how your skin heals. These are the most common factors.

Deep Inflammation

Cystic or nodular acne reaches deeper into the skin. The deeper and longer the inflammation, the more likely it is to damage collagen and leave a scar.

Delayed Acne Treatment

Acne that stays active for months or years gives scarring more chances to develop. Treating breakouts early is one of the best ways to prevent scars.

Picking & Squeezing

Picking or squeezing spots pushes inflammation deeper and injures the surrounding skin, which increases the risk of marks and scars.

Healing Response & Genetics

Some people naturally form scars more easily. Skin type, genetics and how your skin repairs itself all influence how acne heals.

CARING FOR YOUR SKIN

Skin Care Do’s & Don’ts

Good daily habits will not remove existing scars, but they help prevent new ones and support your treatment results.

Do

  • Treat active acne early, with medical guidance
  • Use a broad-spectrum sunscreen every day
  • Choose gentle, non-comedogenic skincare
  • Follow your aftercare advice between sessions

Avoid

  • Picking, squeezing or popping spots
  • Harsh scrubs on inflamed or healing skin
  • Unsupervised strong peels or at-home devices
  • Long sun exposure on recently treated skin

When to See a Doctor

If acne keeps leaving new marks, over-the-counter products have not helped after two to three months, or scars are affecting your confidence, a dermatologist can identify your scar type and plan treatment.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before acne scar treatment.

What are acne scars?
+
Acne scars are lasting changes in the skin that can develop after acne-related inflammation heals. They may appear as depressions, uneven texture or other visible changes.
What is the best treatment for acne scars?
+
There is no single treatment suitable for every scar. The appropriate option depends on the type, depth and severity of scarring and your individual skin condition.
Can acne scars be completely removed?
+
Treatment can significantly improve the appearance of many scars, but complete removal cannot always be guaranteed. The main goal is to make scars less noticeable and improve skin texture.
How many sessions will I need?
+
The number of sessions depends on the type and severity of scarring and the treatment selected. Some scars may require multiple sessions for progressive improvement.
Can laser treatment help acne scars?
+
Selected laser treatments can help improve certain types of acne scars and uneven skin texture when appropriately recommended.
Can active acne and acne scars be treated together?
+
Active acne and scarring are different concerns, but they can sometimes be addressed as part of an overall treatment plan. Controlling active acne is also important to help prevent new scars.
Is there downtime after treatment?
+
Downtime depends on the procedure performed. Some treatments may cause temporary redness, sensitivity, swelling or peeling, while others may require minimal recovery.
How long does it take to see results?
+
Improvement is usually gradual. The skin continues its natural healing and remodeling process after treatment, so results may become more noticeable over time.
Can acne scars come back?
+
A treated scar generally does not return in exactly the same form. However, new acne breakouts can result in new marks or scars, making ongoing acne management important.
