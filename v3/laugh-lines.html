Laugh Lines (Nasolabial Folds)

CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Laugh Lines

Laugh lines, or nasolabial folds, run from the sides of the nose to the corners of the mouth. Everyone has them to some degree, but they can deepen with age as the cheeks lose volume and the skin loses firmness.

Deeper folds are usually a sign of changes in the mid-face rather than a problem with the line itself. A personalised assessment looks at the whole face to plan a natural-looking improvement.

WHY IT HAPPENS

What Causes Laugh Lines?

Laugh lines deepen gradually as several changes happen in the face. These are the most common factors.

Volume Loss in the Cheeks

As the cheeks lose fat and bone support with age, tissue moves downward and the folds beside the mouth deepen.

Collagen & Elasticity Loss

Collagen and elastin decline over time, so the skin becomes less firm and creases settle more easily.

Facial Expressions

Smiling and talking fold the skin in the same place thousands of times, gradually etching the lines deeper.

Sun & Lifestyle

Sun exposure, smoking and significant weight changes can all speed up the loss of skin support.

CARING FOR YOUR SKIN

Skin Care Do’s & Don’ts

Laugh lines are a natural part of expression. These habits help protect skin quality and support your results.

Do

  • Wear broad-spectrum sunscreen every day
  • Keep your skin hydrated and moisturised
  • Eat a balanced, protein-rich diet
  • Sleep on your back where possible

Avoid

  • Smoking, which speeds up collagen loss
  • Rapid weight loss and weight gain
  • Long, unprotected sun exposure
  • Overfilling one line instead of treating the cause

When to See a Doctor

If deepening folds make you look tired or older than you feel, a doctor can assess your mid-face and explain which options would give a natural, balanced result.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about laugh lines.

What causes laugh lines?
+
Laugh lines deepen as the cheeks lose volume, collagen declines and repeated expressions fold the skin. Sun exposure and lifestyle can speed this up.
Can laugh lines be removed completely?
+
Laugh lines are a natural part of the face, so the aim is to soften them rather than erase them. A natural result keeps your expressions intact.
Are fillers the only option?
+
No. Depending on the cause, skin tightening with HIFU or MNRF, or a combined non- invasive face lift, may be recommended instead of or alongside fillers.
Will treatment look natural?
+
The aim is balance. By supporting the mid-face rather than overfilling the fold itself, results usually look refreshed rather than changed.
How long do results last?
+
This depends on the treatment. Fillers often last 12–18 months, while collagen- based tightening develops over months and can last a year or more.
Is there downtime?
+
Most options have little downtime. Fillers can cause mild swelling or bruising for a few days, and energy-based treatments may cause brief redness.
