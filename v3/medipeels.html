CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Medipeel Treatment

Medipeels are professional skin treatments designed to improve the appearance, texture and overall quality of the skin through controlled exfoliation. They can help remove dull surface cells, support skin renewal and address selected concerns such as uneven tone, pigmentation, rough texture and lack of radiance.

The type of peel and treatment intensity are selected according to your skin condition, sensitivity and individual goals. A professional assessment helps determine whether a Medipeel is appropriate for your skin.

THE TREATMENT

Understanding Medipeels

A Medipeel uses carefully selected peeling agents to exfoliate the outer layers of the skin in a controlled manner. This process encourages the skin to renew itself and can help reveal a smoother and more refreshed appearance.

Different peels can be selected for different skin concerns. The treatment approach depends on factors such as skin type, pigmentation, texture, sensitivity and previous skincare treatments.

CLINICAL PROCESS

Process of Medipeel Treatment

Assessment & Planning

Your skin’s condition, concerns and sensitivity are examined, and the appropriate peel is chosen for your skin type and desired outcome.

Preparation & Application

The skin is cleansed and prepared, then the selected peel is applied for an appropriate time under careful professional supervision.

Removal & Soothing

The peel is removed or neutralised according to its type, and suitable products may be applied to comfort and support the skin.

Aftercare

You receive instructions on skincare, sun protection and other precautions to support your skin during its recovery and renewal.

OUTCOMES & BENEFITS

Benefits of Medipeel Treatment

Medipeel treatment may help improve the overall appearance and texture of the skin. Depending on the selected peel, potential benefits include:

Smoother-looking skin
Improved skin texture
More even-looking skin tone
Reduced appearance of surface pigmentation
Improved skin radiance
Removal of dull surface cells
Refreshed and healthier-looking skin

The results can vary depending on the individual’s skin condition and the type of peel performed.

REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS

What Results Can You Expect?

Following treatment, the skin may appear fresher, smoother and more radiant. Depending on the peel selected, you may also notice improvements in uneven tone, surface pigmentation and rough texture.

Some peels may produce mild peeling or flaking as the skin renews itself. Results can develop progressively, and multiple sessions may sometimes be recommended depending on the concern being treated.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Medipeel?
+
A Medipeel is a professional chemical peel treatment that uses selected exfoliating ingredients to remove damaged surface cells and support skin renewal.
Is a Medipeel suitable for every skin type?
+
Different peels can be selected for different skin conditions, but not every peel is suitable for everyone. A skin assessment is recommended before treatment.
How long does a Medipeel treatment take?
+
The duration depends on the type of peel and the treatment plan. The procedure is generally completed within a relatively short clinic visit.
Does a Medipeel hurt?
+
Some people may experience mild tingling, warmth or temporary sensitivity during treatment. The level of discomfort depends on the type and strength of the peel.
Is there any peeling after treatment?
+
Some treatments may cause mild dryness, flaking or peeling, while others may involve little visible peeling. The expected response depends on the formulation used.
How many sessions are required?
+
The number of sessions depends on your skin concerns, treatment type and desired results. A personalised schedule can be recommended after assessment.
Can I use my regular skincare after a Medipeel?
+
Your skincare routine may need to be adjusted temporarily after treatment. You should follow the aftercare instructions provided for your skin.
Is sun protection necessary after treatment?
+
Yes. Protecting the skin from excessive sun exposure is an important part of post-treatment care and can help support the recovery process.
