Mesotherapy in Delhi & Gurgaon

THE TREATMENT

What Is Mesotherapy

Mesotherapy is a series of superficial micro-injections that deliver hyaluronic acid, vitamins, amino acids and antioxidants directly into the mid-dermis — the layer where skin actually holds its water and builds its collagen.

It adds no volume and changes no structure. What it changes is skin quality: hydration, elasticity, tone and the way light sits on the surface. That distinction matters, because mesotherapy is often sold as an alternative to filler when the two do entirely different jobs.

How it works

Topical creams reach the outermost layer of skin and largely stop there. Injecting the same actives past that barrier puts them where the fibroblasts are, and the micro-injury of each entry point triggers a mild wound-healing response of its own. The result comes from both together — the ingredients and the stimulus.

What it will not do

It will not lift sagging skin, fill a deep line, or replace lost facial volume. If those are your concerns, mesotherapy is the wrong tool and we will say so at consultation rather than sell you a course of it.

INDICATIONS

What Mesotherapy Treats

Six concerns where it genuinely earns its place in a plan — and where the evidence for it is strongest.

Dull, Dehydrated Skin

The primary indication. Hyaluronic acid placed in the dermis holds water where creams cannot reach, and the change in light reflection is usually the first thing people notice.

Fine Lines & Crepiness

Superficial lines that come from dehydration rather than muscle movement. Better hydrated skin plumps them from within instead of freezing them.

Loss of Elasticity

Early laxity where skin has begun to feel thinner. The micro-injury response supports gradual collagen activity over the course.

Uneven Tone

Antioxidant and vitamin blends help with sallowness and mild pigmentation, usually alongside a dedicated pigment protocol rather than instead of one.

Neck & Décolletage

Thinner skin that ages faster and is routinely left out of a routine. It responds well and is often treated in the same session as the face.

Scalp & Hair Density

Scalp mesotherapy supports follicle health and is frequently combined with PRP or GFC, following a diagnosis of why hair is being lost.

WHAT TO EXPECT

The Protocol

Start to finish, including the parts that are inconvenient. You should know both before you book, not after.

  1. Step one · Consultation

    Assessment and diagnosis

    Skin analysis, medical history and baseline imaging. We confirm mesotherapy is the right answer for what you have described — and say so plainly if it is not.

  2. Step two · On the day

    Preparation

    Cleansing, then topical anaesthetic for around 20 minutes. The blend is selected for your skin rather than drawn from a single house formula.

  3. Step three · 20 – 30 minutes

    The injections

    A grid of very fine, very shallow injections across the treatment area. Most people describe pinpricks rather than pain; the upper lip and jawline are the most sensitive points.

  4. Step four · Same day

    Immediately after

    Small raised bumps at each entry point, settling within four to six hours. No make-up, no heat, no strenuous exercise for 24 hours. Most people go back to work straight away.

  5. Step five · Weeks 3 – 12

    The course

    Three to four sessions spaced three to four weeks apart. Change is usually visible from the second and continues building through the fourth.

  6. Step six · Month 4 onward

    Review and maintenance

    Reviewed against your baseline images rather than memory. Maintenance is typically a single session every four to six months.

QUESTIONS

Mesotherapy FAQs

Is mesotherapy painful?

Most people describe it as a series of quick pinpricks rather than pain. A topical anaesthetic is applied for 20 minutes beforehand, and the needles used are very fine and very shallow. Sensitivity is highest around the upper lip and jawline.

How many mesotherapy sessions will I need?

Typically three to four sessions spaced three to four weeks apart, followed by maintenance every four to six months. Skin that is very dehydrated or sun-damaged may need a longer initial course, which is decided at review rather than promised upfront.

What is the downtime after mesotherapy?

Small raised bumps at each injection point settle within four to six hours. Mild redness can last until the next morning. Bruising is uncommon but possible, particularly under the eyes. Most people return to work the same day and avoid photographs that evening.

How is mesotherapy different from skin boosters or fillers?

Filler adds volume and structure in a specific place. Mesotherapy adds no volume — it delivers hydration and nutrients across an area to improve skin quality. Skin boosters sit between the two: a similar depth to mesotherapy, but usually hyaluronic acid alone rather than a vitamin and amino acid blend.

Can mesotherapy help with hair loss?

Scalp mesotherapy can support hair density by improving the condition of the scalp and delivering nutrients to the follicle. It works best alongside a diagnosis of why hair is being lost, and is frequently combined with PRP or GFC rather than used alone.

Who should not have mesotherapy?

It is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, over active skin infection or inflamed acne, or where there is a known allergy to any component of the blend. Anyone on blood thinners or with a bleeding disorder needs medical clearance first.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Suitability, expected outcomes and risks can only be established at an in-person consultation with a qualified practitioner. Individual results vary.
