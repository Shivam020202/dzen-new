CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Botox Treatment

Botox is a non-surgical treatment used to temporarily reduce the appearance of certain facial lines and wrinkles. It works by relaxing specific muscles responsible for repeated facial movements, helping create a smoother and more refreshed appearance.

The treatment is planned according to facial movement, muscle activity, skin condition and individual aesthetic goals. A professional consultation helps determine the appropriate areas and treatment approach.

THE TREATMENT

Understanding Botox Treatment

Facial expressions such as smiling, frowning and squinting repeatedly contract certain muscles. Over time, these movements can contribute to the development of expression lines.

Botox works by temporarily reducing the activity of selected muscles. This can soften the appearance of dynamic wrinkles while allowing for a natural-looking result when appropriately administered.

The treatment is customised according to individual facial anatomy and the desired outcome.

CLINICAL PROCESS

Process of Botox Treatment

Facial Assessment & Planning

Your facial movements, muscle activity, lines and skin are assessed, and the treatment areas and approach are planned around your goals.

Treatment Preparation

The treatment area is carefully cleaned and prepared before the procedure, so the skin is clean and ready for each precise injection.

Precise Injections

Small amounts of Botox are carefully injected into selected muscles using a fine needle, following the plan made for your facial anatomy.

Aftercare & Follow-Up

Simple precautions are explained to support the treatment, and a follow-up may be recommended to review your response and any adjustment.

REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS

What Results Can You Expect?

Botox gradually reduces the activity of treated muscles, which can soften the appearance of certain expression lines. The skin may appear smoother and the face more refreshed.

Results are temporary and develop progressively rather than immediately. The duration of results varies between individuals and depends on factors such as the treatment area, muscle activity and individual response.

Maintaining a natural appearance is an important part of treatment planning, and the amount used should be appropriate for the desired outcome.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Botox?
+
Botox is an injectable treatment that temporarily reduces the activity of selected muscles. It is commonly used to soften certain expression lines and wrinkles.
Is Botox a surgical treatment?
+
No. Botox is a non-surgical injectable treatment performed using a fine needle.
Does Botox treatment hurt?
+
Most people experience only brief discomfort during the injections. The sensation can vary depending on the treatment area and individual sensitivity.
How long does Botox take to work?
+
The effects develop gradually after treatment. The timing can vary between individuals and treatment areas.
How long do Botox results last?
+
Results are temporary. How long they remain visible depends on factors such as the treatment area, muscle activity and individual response.
Is there downtime after Botox?
+
Botox generally involves minimal downtime. Some people may experience temporary redness, swelling or small injection-site marks.
Can Botox look natural?
+
When appropriately planned and administered, Botox can provide a subtle and natural-looking improvement while preserving normal facial expression.
Can Botox be used for every wrinkle?
+
Botox primarily works on wrinkles associated with muscle movement. Wrinkles caused mainly by skin laxity, volume loss or other factors may require a different treatment approach.
When can I return to my normal activities?
+
Many people can resume normal daily activities shortly after treatment. Specific post-treatment precautions should be followed according to the guidance provided by your doctor.
Is Botox suitable for everyone?
+
Botox may not be appropriate for every individual. A consultation is important to assess your medical history, facial anatomy, treatment goals and suitability before proceeding.
