Hydrafacial

CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Understanding Hydrafacial

Precision-led skin resurfacing and vortex hydration crafted to restore radiance from within.

Hydrafacial is a non-invasive skin treatment designed to cleanse, exfoliate and hydrate the skin in a single session. It combines multiple skincare steps to help remove surface impurities while supporting hydration and a smoother, fresher-looking complexion.

At D’Zen Derma, Hydrafacial can be customised according to your skin type and concerns. It can be considered for dull-looking skin, uneven texture, dehydration, congested pores and lack of radiance.

If you are looking for Hydrafacial in Gurgaon, a consultation can help determine the treatment approach best suited to your skin.

CLINICAL MECHANISM

How Does Hydrafacial Work?

A Hydrafacial treatment follows a multi-step approach to refresh and revitalise the skin.

Cleansing & Exfoliation

The HydraFacial tip moves across the skin, removing dead cells, surface impurities and excess oil through a gentle, controlled exfoliation. The skin is cleared and primed for the steps that follow.

Acid Peel

A gentle AHA-BHA complex is applied to loosen the contents of congested pores. The formula is calibrated not to cause irritation or visible peeling.

Extraction

The suction removes blackheads and impurities from pores painlessly. Clients typically describe a mild pulling sensation rather than any discomfort.

Serum Infusion

Antioxidants, hyaluronic acid, and any targeted boosters selected for your skin are infused into the skin. Absorption at this point is significantly enhanced because the skin has been thoroughly cleared.

Customised Skin Care

Depending on your skin requirements, the treatment can be tailored to focus on hydration, radiance, texture or other specific concerns.

The result is a treatment that combines cleansing, exfoliation, extraction and hydration in one streamlined facial procedure.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your Hydrafacial session in Gurgaon.

Is Hydrafacial suitable for all skin types?
+
Hydrafacial can be customised according to individual skin requirements. A skin assessment is recommended to determine the most suitable approach for you. Serum strength and peel formulation are adjusted for sensitive skin at consultation, and the treatment is well tolerated across most skin types, including reactive and rosacea-prone skin.
Is Hydrafacial painful?
+
Hydrafacial is generally designed to be a comfortable, non-invasive treatment. Individual comfort can vary depending on skin sensitivity and the treatment approach used. Most clients describe a gentle, cooling suction sensation.
Is there downtime after Hydrafacial?
+
Hydrafacial is a non-invasive treatment with zero downtime. You can immediately return to your daily routine or attend an evening event with glowing skin.
Can Hydrafacial help with dull skin?
+
Yes. The combination of cleansing, exfoliation, and hydration immediately clears superficial dead cell buildup, giving dull skin a brighter, revitalized appearance.
Can I get a Hydrafacial before an event or Marriage?
+
Yes. HydraFacial is often chosen as a pre-event treatment because there is no downtime. Two to three days before the occasion is generally recommended, so the skin has time to settle into its most radiant state.
How often should I get a Hydrafacial?
+
Once a month is the standard recommendation for maintaining skin health. Your dermatologist may suggest more frequent sessions for active acne or specific concerns, based on your consultation.
What skin concerns does HydraFacial address?
+
Acne, blackheads, enlarged pores, uneven texture, dullness, dehydration, pigmentation, and early signs of ageing.
