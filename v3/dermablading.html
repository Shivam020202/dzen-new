CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Dermablading Treatment

Dermablading is a non-invasive skin treatment that uses a sterile, specialised blade to gently remove the outermost layer of dead skin cells and fine facial hair. This can leave the skin feeling smoother, softer and more refreshed.

The treatment is performed with controlled movements according to the condition and sensitivity of the skin. A professional assessment helps determine whether dermablading is suitable for your skin.

THE TREATMENT

Understanding Dermablading

Dermablading focuses on gentle surface exfoliation and removal of fine facial hair. By clearing away accumulated dead skin cells, the treatment can create a smoother surface and improve the overall appearance of the skin.

It may also help skincare products spread more evenly across the skin. The procedure does not permanently change the nature or thickness of facial hair.

CLINICAL PROCESS

Process of Dermablading Treatment

Assessment & Preparation

Your skin’s condition and sensitivity are assessed, then the area is thoroughly cleansed to remove makeup, oil and surface impurities.

Controlled Dermablading

A sterile blade is carefully moved across the skin at a controlled angle to remove dead surface cells and fine facial hair.

Skin Soothing

Suitable soothing or hydrating products may be applied after the procedure to help keep the skin calm, comfortable and hydrated.

Protective Care & Follow-Up

Sun protection and skincare advice support the skin after treatment, and a review can confirm whether another session is suitable.

Benefits of Dermablading Treatment

Dermablading can provide several cosmetic benefits, including:

Smoother-feeling skin

Removal of fine facial hair
Fresher-looking complexion

REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS

What Results Can You Expect?

Following dermablading, the skin may feel noticeably smoother and appear fresher. Removing surface buildup and fine facial hair can create a more even-looking skin surface.

The results are temporary because the skin naturally accumulates dead cells and fine facial hair grows back over time. Maintaining an appropriate skincare routine can help support the appearance of the skin between treatments.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is dermablading?
+
Dermablading is a non-invasive exfoliation treatment that uses a sterile blade to remove dead surface skin cells and fine facial hair.
Does dermablading make facial hair grow back thicker?
+
No. Dermablading does not change the thickness, colour or growth rate of facial hair. The hair grows back according to its natural characteristics.
Is dermablading painful?
+
The procedure is generally well tolerated. Some people may experience mild sensitivity or a scraping sensation during treatment.
Is there any downtime?
+
Dermablading generally involves minimal downtime. Mild redness or sensitivity may occur temporarily after treatment.
Can I wear makeup after dermablading?
+
It may be advisable to allow the skin some time to settle before applying makeup. Follow the aftercare instructions provided after your treatment.
How often can dermablading be done?
+
The ideal frequency depends on your skin condition and individual goals. A suitable schedule can be recommended after assessing your skin.
