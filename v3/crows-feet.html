Crow's Feet

CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Crow’s Feet

Crow's feet are the fine lines that fan out from the outer corners of the eyes. They first appear when you smile or squint and, over time, can remain visible even when your face is relaxed.

Lines that only appear with expression respond to different treatment than lines that are always visible. A personalised assessment identifies which type you have and the most natural way to soften them.

WHY IT HAPPENS

What Causes Crow’s Feet?

Crow's feet form gradually from movement and skin ageing. These are the most common factors.

Repeated Expressions

Smiling, laughing and squinting fold the thin skin around the eyes in the same place many times a day.

Thin Skin

The skin around the eyes is among the thinnest on the body, so lines form there early.

Collagen Loss

Collagen and elastin decline with age, so expression lines start to stay visible at rest.

Sun Exposure

Sun damage speeds up skin ageing, and squinting in bright light adds to line formation.

CARING FOR YOUR SKIN

Skin Care Do’s & Don’ts

Protecting the delicate skin around the eyes helps slow new lines and maintain results.

Do

  • Wear sunglasses in bright light
  • Use sunscreen around the eyes
  • Apply a gentle, hydrating eye cream
  • Get regular, good-quality sleep

Avoid

  • Squinting at screens or in sunlight
  • Rubbing the eyes
  • Smoking, which speeds up skin ageing
  • Harsh products on thin eye skin

When to See a Doctor

If lines around the eyes are making you look tired or older than you feel, a doctor can assess whether they are expression lines, static lines or both, and recommend the most natural option.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about crow's feet.

What causes crow's feet?
+
Crow's feet are caused by repeated smiling and squinting, thin skin around the eyes, collagen loss with age and sun exposure.
Does Botox work for crow's feet?
+
Botox is commonly used to soften crow's feet that appear with expression. Results usually show within about two weeks.
Will I still be able to smile naturally?
+
Yes. When dosed carefully, treatment softens lines while keeping a natural, expressive smile.
How long do results last?
+
Anti-wrinkle injections usually last around three to four months, while skin- quality treatments build gradually and can last longer.
Can lines that are always visible be treated?
+
Yes. Static lines often respond best to skin-quality treatments such as MNRF or mesotherapy, sometimes combined with Botox.
Is there downtime?
+
Downtime is usually minimal. Small bumps or redness after injections settle quickly, and bruising is uncommon.
