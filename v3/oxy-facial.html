CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Oxy-Facial Treatment

Oxy-Facial is a non-invasive facial treatment designed to refresh the skin, improve hydration and enhance its overall appearance. It combines skin cleansing and exfoliation with nourishing ingredients to help remove surface impurities and leave the skin looking smoother and more radiant.

The treatment can be customised according to your skin condition and individual concerns. A professional skin assessment helps determine the most suitable approach for your skin.

THE TREATMENT

Understanding Oxy-Facial

An Oxy-Facial is designed to provide a refreshing treatment experience without the need for invasive procedures or significant downtime. It focuses on cleansing the skin, removing surface buildup and supporting hydration.

Depending on the treatment protocol, it may include exfoliation, infusion of skin-supporting ingredients and oxygen-based treatment to leave the skin looking refreshed and revitalised.

CLINICAL PROCESS

Process of Oxy-Facial Treatment

Skin Assessment

Your skin is carefully assessed to understand its condition, sensitivity, hydration levels and specific concerns, so the facial suits your skin.

Cleansing & Exfoliation

The skin is thoroughly cleansed of impurities, excess oil and makeup, then gently exfoliated to remove dead skin cells and improve smoothness.

Infusion & Oxygen Treatment

Selected ingredients are infused for hydration and nourishment, followed by an oxygen-based treatment for a refreshing, revitalising feel.

Finishing Care

Suitable moisturising and protective products are applied to leave the skin comfortable and refreshed, followed by simple aftercare guidance.

REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS

What Results Can You Expect?

After an Oxy-Facial, the skin may appear cleaner, smoother, hydrated and more refreshed. Many people notice an immediate improvement in the skin’s overall appearance and radiance.

The results are generally cosmetic and can vary depending on your skin condition. Regular professional treatments combined with an appropriate skincare routine may help maintain a healthy-looking appearance.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Oxy-Facial?
+
An Oxy-Facial is a non-invasive facial treatment that combines cleansing, exfoliation, hydration and oxygen-based care to refresh and revitalise the skin.
Is an Oxy-Facial suitable for all skin types?
+
The treatment can be adapted for different skin conditions, but suitability depends on individual skin sensitivity and concerns. A professional assessment is recommended.
Does an Oxy-Facial hurt?
+
The treatment is generally gentle and comfortable. You may experience mild sensations during cleansing, exfoliation or other treatment steps.
Is there any downtime?
+
Oxy-Facial is generally associated with minimal downtime. Your skin may look refreshed immediately after treatment, although individual responses can vary.
How often can I get an Oxy-Facial?
+
The ideal frequency depends on your skin condition, skincare routine and treatment goals. Your skin professional can recommend an appropriate schedule.
Can I apply makeup after an Oxy-Facial?
+
This depends on your skin’s condition and the products used during treatment. You should follow the specific aftercare advice provided after your session.
How long do the results last?
+
The visible freshness and hydration can vary from person to person. Maintaining a suitable skincare routine can help support the appearance of the skin between treatments.
Can an Oxy-Facial replace medical skin treatments?
+
An Oxy-Facial is primarily a cosmetic skin-refreshing treatment. Persistent or specific skin concerns may require assessment and treatment by a qualified skin professional.
