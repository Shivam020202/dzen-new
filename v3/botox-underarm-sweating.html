Botox for Underarm Sweating

CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Botox for Underarm Sweating

Botox for underarm sweating is a quick, minimally invasive treatment for excessive sweating, known as hyperhidrosis. Small amounts of botulinum toxin are injected just beneath the skin to reduce the nerve signals that activate the sweat glands.

The same approach can be used for the palms or feet where suitable. A doctor's assessment confirms the cause of excessive sweating and whether this treatment is right for you.

THE TREATMENT

Understanding Botox for Sweating

Sweat glands are switched on by a chemical messenger released from nearby nerves. Botox temporarily blocks this signal in the treated area, so the glands produce much less sweat, while the rest of the body continues to sweat and cool normally.

The effect is temporary and wears off as the nerve endings recover, usually after several months. If sweating is sudden, new or affects the whole body, an underlying cause is checked first.

CLINICAL PROCESS

Process of Botox for Sweating

Assessment

Your sweating pattern and medical history are assessed to confirm the cause and the right treatment.

Mapping

The area is cleansed and, where needed, a simple starch-iodine test maps where sweating is most active.

Micro-Injections

A series of very fine, shallow injections is placed across the area. The procedure usually takes under 30 minutes.

Aftercare

You can usually return to your routine straight away, avoiding heavy exercise and heat for around 24 hours.

Benefits of Botox for Sweating

Botox for sweating can provide several benefits depending on your concern and treatment area:

Noticeable reduction in underarm sweating
Fewer sweat marks on clothing
Reduced underarm odour for many people
Quick, minimally invasive procedure
Little or no downtime
Results that last several months

REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS

What Results Can You Expect?

Sweating usually starts to reduce within a few days, with the full effect seen at around two weeks.

Results commonly last around six months, sometimes longer, before sweating gradually returns. Repeat treatments can be planned when needed.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to stop sweating in one area?
+
Yes. Only a small proportion of the body's sweat glands are in the underarms, so the body continues to regulate its temperature normally.
How long does Botox for sweating last?
+
Results commonly last around six months and can last longer for some people. Repeat treatments are planned when sweating returns.
Do the injections hurt?
+
The injections are very shallow and the needle is fine, so most people find underarm treatment quite tolerable. Palms and soles are more sensitive and may need extra numbing.
Will it reduce body odour?
+
Odour comes from bacteria breaking down sweat on the skin, so reducing sweat often reduces odour as well, although this varies.
When will I see results?
+
Sweating usually starts to reduce within a few days, with the full effect at around two weeks.
Who should not have this treatment?
+
It is not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding, with certain neuromuscular conditions, or with infection in the treatment area. Your doctor will review your medical history first.
