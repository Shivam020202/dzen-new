Protocols, Not Packages

THE DEFINITION

What Makes a Protocol a Signature

A signature is not a treatment with a name on it. It is a sequence — a defined order of steps, spaced at defined intervals, chosen because that order produces a result the individual treatments cannot produce alone.

Anyone can sell you a session. What takes longer to build is knowing which session, in what order, with how long in between — and which one to leave out. That judgement is what these four marks describe.

01 Sequenced, Not Bundled Each step is scheduled to land while the previous one is still working. Order is the active ingredient.
02 Measured Against a Baseline Imaging at the start, reviewed at fixed points. Progress is compared, not remembered.
03 Defined End Point Every protocol has a point at which it stops and becomes maintenance. Open-ended treatment is not a plan.
04 Inside and Outside Together Sleep, stress and nutrition are written into the protocol, because they decide how much of the clinic work holds.

THE TWO HALVES

Aesthetics + Wellness

Every signature runs both columns at once. Treat one without the other and the result holds for a season instead of a decade.

Outside

What the clinic does

  • Diagnosis and baseline imaging
  • Correction — pigment, texture, scarring
  • Collagen stimulation and bio-remodelling
  • Injectables, placed sparingly
  • Maintenance at defined intervals
Inside

What decides whether it holds

  • Sleep quality and cortisol load
  • Nutrition and gut health
  • Bloodwork where it is indicated
  • Barrier-respecting home routine
  • Stress protocols around key dates

Beauty is what balance looks like from the outside. We work on both halves because only one of them is visible — and it is not the one that decides the outcome.
