MEN'S AESTHETICS

Looking Like You, Just Rested

THE APPROACH

Built for Male Anatomy

Male skin is measurably different — roughly 20% thicker, denser in collagen, higher in sebum, and carrying more muscle mass under it. A plan borrowed from a female protocol and scaled down does not account for any of that.

The goal is almost always the same when men come in: look less tired, not different. That is a narrower brief than it sounds, and it is easy to overshoot. Everything here is built to stay on the right side of that line.

Different Dosing, Not Less Greater muscle mass needs higher neuromodulator dosing, and thicker skin needs different laser settings. Under-dosing is why results disappoint.
Structure Kept Angular Filler is placed to hold a square jaw and flat cheek, not to round them. The placement map is genuinely different.
Low Downtime by Default Protocols are sequenced around the assumption that you are back at work tomorrow, with nothing visible to explain.
Nothing Announced The brief is that no one can point to what changed — only that you look better rested than you did.

Matched to the Concern
