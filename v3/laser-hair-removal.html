Laser Hair Removal

Less Hair, Less Maintenance. More Freedom

Understanding Laser Hair Removal

Laser Hair Removal, also known as Laser Hair Reduction (LHR), is an advanced treatment designed to reduce unwanted facial and body hair. The procedure uses controlled laser energy to target the hair follicle and gradually reduce hair growth over a series of sessions.

Unlike shaving, waxing or threading, which provide temporary hair removal, laser treatment works at the follicular level to achieve longer-lasting hair reduction. With a personalised treatment plan, hair can become progressively finer, less dense and slower growing over time.

At D’Zen Derma, Laser Hair Reduction is personalised to your skin, hair and treatment area — using quad-wavelength diode technology and carefully selected treatment parameters for progressive, long-term hair reduction.

BIOLOGICAL MECHANISM

How Does Laser Hair Reduction Work – The Working Principle?

Laser Hair Reduction uses focused laser energy to target the pigment present in the hair follicle. The energy is absorbed by the hair and converted into controlled thermal energy, which acts on the follicle and reduces its ability to produce hair.

The treated hair gradually sheds over the following 2–3 weeks. Since hair follicles are naturally present in different stages of their growth cycle, multiple sessions are needed to progressively target the follicles during their suitable growth phase.

CLINICAL RESULTS

What Results Can You Expect?

LHR is a gradual process. With each session, treated hair typically becomes finer, less dense and slower to return. Many people also find themselves shaving or waxing less frequently.

With a planned course of treatment, you may notice:

✓ Reduced hair density ✓ Finer hair regrowth ✓ Slower visible hair growth ✓ Smoother-looking skin ✓ Longer intervals between shaving or waxing ✓ Progressive improvement with successive sessions

For Indian skin types, an average of 6–8 sessions may be required. The exact number varies according to the treatment area, hair thickness, skin and hair colour and individual response.

Treatment Progression:

After 2–3 Sessions · Early Changes
Some patients may begin noticing visible hair reduction after 2–3 sessions, while more significant results develop over the recommended treatment course.
Course of 6–8 Sessions · Significant Reduction
Hair becomes progressively finer, less dense and slower to return with optimal cumulative reduction.
Hormonal & Maintenance Considerations
Hormonal factors can influence hair regrowth. Patients with underlying hormonal concerns may require additional or maintenance sessions as advised by the doctor.
CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Laser Hair Reduction at D'Zen Derma.

1. How many sessions of Laser Hair Reduction will I need?
+
Many patients may require approximately 6–8 sessions. The number can vary depending on the treatment area, hair thickness, skin and hair characteristics and individual response. Our Gold standard Quad Wavelength technology machine ensures maximum results in lesser number of sessions.
2. How frequently should I have my sessions?
+
Sessions are generally spaced around 3–6 weeks apart, depending on the treatment area and individual hair-growth cycle.
3. Does Laser Hair Reduction hurt?
+
The treatment is designed to be comfortable, with cooling technology used during the procedure. The sensation may vary depending on the treatment area, hair thickness and individual sensitivity. Our Gold standard Quad Wavelength technology machine ensures maximum comfort through its cooling technology.
4. When will I see results?
+
Hair reduction develops gradually. Some patients may begin noticing visible changes after 2–3 sessions, with further improvement as the treatment course progresses.
5. Is there any downtime after Laser Hair Reduction?
+
Laser Hair Reduction is a relatively quick treatment, and patients can generally return to their regular activities after the session. Following the recommended aftercare is important for maintaining skin comfort.
6. Can hormonal factors affect the results?
+
Yes, hormonal imbalances or conditions like PCOS, Hyperandrogenism, High prolactin levels may affect hair regrowth and could require additional maintenance sessions.
7. Can Laser Hair Reduction be done on darker skin?
+
The Quad-Wavelength technology at D’Zen Derma is designed for different skin types, including darker skin. Treatment parameters are selected according to individual skin and hair characteristics.
8. What should I avoid after treatment?
+
Follow the aftercare instructions provided by your doctor. Generally, prolonged sun exposure, aggressive scrubbing, massages, steam and sauna should be avoided for the recommended period. Strong skin actives such as retinol, AHA and BHA products may also need to be paused temporarily.
9. Is Laser Hair Reduction permanent?
+
Laser Hair Reduction provides long-lasting reduction in hair growth, but individual regrowth can occur. Hormonal factors and individual hair-growth patterns may influence long-term results and the need for maintenance treatments.
10. Is Laser Hair Reduction Effective for All Hair types and all skin tones?
+
  • Darker/coarser hair generally responds better.
  • Very fine hair can be more challenging.
  • Blonde/grey/white hair may respond poorly because of lower pigment.
  • Hormonal hair growth may require additional/maintenance sessions.
  • Skin tones: The Quad-Wavelength technology at D'Zen Derma allows parameters to be calibrated across all Fitzpatrick skin tones.
11. Should I get a laser hair removal done from a spa or a clinic?
+
Always choose a medical dermatology clinic. Spas and commercial salons often employ low-powered, rented devices operated by non-medical staff with higher risk of ineffective treatment or burns. At D'Zen Derma, all protocols are 100% Doctor-Administered & Supervised using medical-grade gold standard technology.
12. Who should avoid Laser Hair Reduction?
+
Laser Hair Reduction should be avoided by:
  • Pregnant & Lactating women
  • Active skin infections
  • Inflammatory skin conditions
