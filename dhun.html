Wellness Programs
THE CONCEPT
A wellness reset in the heart of the city

At Dhun Wellness, wellness is not just a destination - it's a deeply immersive journey tailored to restore balance, vitality, and inner harmony. As India’s first in-city wellness center, our signature wellness programs blend time-honored Ayurvedic wisdom with modern therapeutic practices to support healing at every level of your being.

Tailored for the demands of urban life, Dhun Wellness’ signature wellness programs target the root causes of common concerns such as gut imbalances, fatigue, hormonal shifts, weight gain, and disrupted sleep. We see symptoms as signals, not problems,and use root-cause analysis to create personalized treatment plans that restore balance and vitality. With expert therapies, customized take-home care, and a calming environment, Dhun Wellness provides a lasting, holistic approach to true wellbeing -right in the heart of the city.

Sleep Program
3 Days | 5 Days | 7 Days

The Sleep Program at Dhun Wellness is designed to gently realign your sleep cycle, restore balance to your circadian rhythm, and enhance the quality of your rest. Through a curated blend of bespoke treatments, we focus on harmonising the pineal gland, optimising the natural sleep cycle, and ensuring a deep, rejuvenating slumber. The program is complemented with personalised dietary guidance and simple daily practices to continue supporting your wellbeing at home.

Gut Restoration
3 Days | 5 Days | 7 Days

Embark on a transformative journey with Dhun Wellness' Gut Restoration Program, where ancient Ayurvedic wisdom meets modern wellness. Rooted in the time-honoured principles of Panchakarma, it focuses on purging harmful toxins from the gut to restore harmony and balance to your body’s vital functions. The program recognises the health of your microbiome as the cornerstone of vitality and longevity and offers a path to detoxification and the revival of your Body, Mind, and Soul - helping to reverse the underlying causes of lifestyle diseases and elevating your health to its fullest potential.
